Land document verification

One property. A trusted record.

Owners upload a deed or land record for OCR and authorized human review. The authority signs the verified status; private documents remain encrypted. Buyers, banks and lawyers see only owner-approved QR fields.

No government records API is configured in this project. Submissions stay clearly marked Pending until an authorized human reviewer checks the original record. OCR only assists data entry; it does not verify ownership.
